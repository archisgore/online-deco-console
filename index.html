Deco Planner

Decompression, in your browser.

Buhlmann ZHL with Gradient Factors and VPM-B. Multi-gas, multi-level. Free, open-source, no install.

How to use this: Deco Planner runs the same decompression models as the desktop planners (Buhlmann-GF, VPM-B) directly in your browser. Treat results as candidates, not a final plan — cross-check against your trusted desktop planner or tables, and never deviate from your training. This tool can be wrong.

Algorithm & conservatism

Surface breathing rate. ~28 L/min ≈ 1 cu ft/min — a conservative recreational default.

Tip

Defaults give a conservative trimix profile (GF 20/80). Raise GF Low for slower bottom unloading, raise GF High for steeper final ascent. ppO₂ 1.6 is standard deco; drop to 1.4 for rec.
